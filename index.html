Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Does this work on all UPI payment apps in India?

Yes. The QR codes adhere strictly to the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) URI standard (upi://pay?pa=...&pn=...), making them 100% scannable across Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and bank apps.

Does this service store or log my banking information?

No. The entire engine executes 100% inside your local browser. No transaction details, payee VPAs, or payment amounts ever leave your device or touch an external server database.

Can I print physical acrylic countertop standees?

Yes. The studio includes an automated **Print Standee** function formatted for A5/A4 tabletop acrylic stands, as well as a 4x Ultra-Res (2048px) PNG export designed for professional flex and metal plate printing.

How does Gmail login secure my records?

All authentication credentials use client-side SHA-256 Web Cryptography hashes. Your session and batch records remain protected in encrypted browser storage with zero third-party tracking.
